The cost of running Sampaguita is rising, and the additional cost to owners in 2027 and 2028 is material — on the order of USD 9,500–11,000 per villa of new cost in 2027 versus the prior budget year, and again in 2028. These items need owner and Board action before the November 7, 2026 AGM.

  1. Operating budget up about +29% year on year.

    FY2027 proposed at USD 265,270 (MXN 4,642,223) vs a FY2026 budget of approximately USD 205,000 — up roughly USD 60,000. Driven by utilities being brought current after deferred 2025 invoices, the full landscaping contract, payroll taxes and IMSS for all three employees, and the beach concession now funded. Detail in §2. (2025 recorded spending was lower still, about USD 146,000, so the cash increase owners feel will be steeper than the budget-to-budget 29%.)

  2. Dues rise for every villa — permanently.

    Dues are each villa's allocation % of the operating budget, so the increase flows straight through. A 6.81% villa moves from about USD 14,000 to USD 18,065 a year (+~USD 4,100); Villa 14 (9.25%) about +USD 5,600. This is a permanent step-up, not a one-off. Per villa in §3.

  3. 2027 roof assessment — USD 5,357 per villa.

    USD 75,000 (MXN 1,312,500) total, split evenly at USD 5,357 (MXN 93,750) per villa, due June 1, 2027. Recorded as a draft, not yet issued; not funded from the reserve.

  4. A matching 2028 roof assessment is anticipated.

    The roof project totals USD 150,000 — USD 75,000 in 2027 and USD 75,000 in 2028 (again ~USD 5,357 per villa). Not yet raised or approved; budget for it now.

  5. Reserve fund is at its floor — a rebuild assessment is triggered.

    USD 30,000 — only 30% of the USD 100,000 target, and exactly equal to the 30% floor. Under the draft Reserve Fund Policy (Revision 2), sitting at the floor triggers a special assessment to rebuild it. The gap to target is USD 70,000; the policy's 5-year plan implies a top-up of about USD 14,000/yr, while the FY2027 budget carries only USD 10,000 — to reconcile before adopting the budget (§4).

What this means for owners

A permanently higher dues bill (~+USD 4,100/yr for a 6.81% villa, up to ~+USD 5,600 for Villa 14) plus a USD 5,357 roof assessment in 2027 and again in 2028 — and, because the reserve is at its floor, a likely further assessment to rebuild it. Unconfirmed; for planning until the Board adopts the budget and issues the assessments.

Also flagged

  • Property insurance lapses December 30, 2026 — renewal not confirmed in the records.
  • Statutory filings untracked — no consolidated register of the Association's SAT / IMSS / state / municipal obligations; late payments and penalties have resulted.
  • No accountant of record; no prior AGM minutes on file.
