The Association

Sampaguita Villas is a 14-villa beachfront community in San José del Cabo, Baja California Sur, governed by Asociación de Condominios de Sampaguita Villas A.C. ("the HOA") under the Sampaguita Condominium Regime and Bylaws (2013 revision) and the HOA Operations Manual.

This report brings together, in one place, everything an owner would expect to review before the Annual General Meeting: how the community is governed, who manages it, the state of each villa, and the full financial picture — the proposed operating budget, dues, the reserve fund, and the assessments planned for the roof project. All monetary figures are shown in Mexican pesos (MXN) with a US-dollar equivalent at 1 USD = 17.5 MXN.

Status of the numbers

The FY2027 operating budget is a proposed budget for owner approval — not yet adopted. The reserve fund policy referenced throughout is Revision 2, a circulation draft. The reserve target and the 2027–2028 roof assessments are figures to be confirmed or amended by owner motion at the AGM.

Scope

Day-to-day management of the property, facilities and community; hiring and supervision of HOA employees and sub-contractors (pool, grounds, fumigation, irrigation, propane, water system, front security gates); monthly water-meter readings per villa, billed quarterly with a report to owners; banking, accounts payable and payroll (salaries, IMSS, bonuses); the annual Beach Concession payment and renewal; assistance to the Board and Treasurer with the annual Operating and Capital budgets; organising and securing Facturas for Board, accountant and Hacienda review; and legal representation of the HOA within the granted power of attorney. Powers the PM does not hold

Per 2013 By-Law Article VIII, the PM may not levy fines, hold hearings or impose discipline; make capital expenditures; or file suit or record a lien for unpaid assessments. Outside contracts over USD 2,500 require at least three competitive bids.

This section sets out how the meeting is called and conducted. The specific notice periods and voting thresholds must be confirmed against the Sampaguita Condominium Regime and 2013 Bylaws; where this report states a figure it is the common practice and is marked for confirmation.

ChairJane Ellmann
NoticeWritten notice to every owner of record, bilingual (English / Spanish), sent at least [15] days before the meeting — period to be confirmed against the Bylaws. Notice includes the agenda, this report, the proxy form, and the budget.

Quorum

  • First call — a majority of the total ownership interest (> 50% of the indiviso) present in person or by proxy. Threshold to confirm.
  • Second call — if the first call fails, the meeting reconvenes (commonly 30–60 minutes later) and is valid for ordinary business with the owners then present, whatever percentage they represent.
  • Reinforced majorities — certain resolutions require more than a simple majority of those present: adopting or amending the budget where it materially changes dues, special assessments and capital expenditures (which also require prior written notice and an owners' hearing), amendments to the Bylaws or the Condominium Regime, and granting or revoking powers of attorney. The exact percentages (commonly two-thirds or higher) must be confirmed against the governing documents and stated in the notice for each such motion.

Proxies

  • An owner who cannot attend may appoint a proxy in writing (carta poder) using the form circulated with the notice.
  • The proxy must be another owner or a person named by the owner; one person may hold a limited number of proxies (limit to confirm).
  • Signed proxy forms must reach the Secretary before the meeting opens — by email to the HOA address or in person at registration. Proxies are logged in the attendance record and count toward quorum.
  • An owner who later attends in person revokes their proxy automatically.

Agenda

  1. Call to order; confirmation of notice; establishment of quorum (first or second call).
  2. Appointment of the Secretary for the meeting and of scrutineers for the vote.
  3. Approval of the minutes of the previous Annual General Meeting.
  4. President's report.
  5. Treasurer's report — prior-year accounts, audit, and the current cash and reserve position.
  6. Property Manager's report.
  7. Motion — adopt the FY2027 operating budget and the resulting dues (§6–7), with the reserve line reconciled.
  8. Motion — set the reserve fund target and adopt the Reserve Fund Policy, Revision 2 (§8).
  9. Motion — approve the 2027 roof special assessment (§9), and note the intended 2028 assessment.
  10. Motion — confirm the property-management contract extension (§4).
  11. Statutory filings and registrations — status report and assignment of ownership (§3).
  12. Property insurance renewal (§11).
  13. Election / confirmation of Board positions and terms.
  14. Owner motions and agenda items received under the deadline below.
  15. Any other business; date of the next meeting; adjournment.

Any owner may propose a motion or add an item to the agenda.
